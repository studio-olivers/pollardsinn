About the pub

A Grade II listed coach house with a story to tell.

The Bulls Head dates to the mid-eighteenth century. It has been a coaching inn, a meeting place, and a local for countless generations of Davenham. Today we keep the same tradition — a warm fire, good company, and a well-kept pint.

Independent and proudly so, we take care of the details so our patrons don't have to.
