Privacy

How we look after your details.

Pollards Inn, Willaston (“we”, “us”) is an independent pub at Village Square, Willaston, Neston, Cheshire CH64 2TU. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Who we are

For questions about this policy, email info@thepollardsinn.co.uk or call 0151 669 1669.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the pub and stay in touch:

  • Emails and phone calls you send us, including private hire or booking enquiries.
  • Table bookings made through OpenTable, which has its own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your details to take bookings and answer enquiries. We do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

We use trusted services to run the site and take bookings:

  • OpenTable, if you book a table online
  • Google, for the map on our Contact page
  • Meta / Facebook, if you follow or message us there

Those companies process information under their own terms. We do not run advertising pixels on this website.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as we need them to help you or to keep a simple record of the conversation.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time using the link in our emails, or by contacting us directly.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website itself does not set marketing cookies. Embedded tools, including Google Maps and OpenTable if you follow a booking link, may set their own cookies so those services work. You can control cookies in your browser settings.
